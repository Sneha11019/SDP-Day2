Workshop Introduction

Welcome to our Engineering Workshop! This section is dedicated to training students in basic mechanical and electrical tools. Here, students get practical knowledge on how to operate various hand tools, power tools, and workshop machinery correctly and safely.

Engineering Tools

Lathe Machine

Use: Used for cutting, turning, knurling, and drilling metal workpieces by rotating them against a cutting tool.

Milling Machine

Use: Used to machine flat and uneven surfaces by removing material using a rotating cutter.

Welding Machine

Use: Used to join two metal parts together by melting the pieces using electrical arc heat.

Digital Multimeter

Use: Used for measuring electrical properties like voltage, current, and resistance in circuits.

Safety Rules

Always follow these rules inside the engineering workshop to avoid accidents:

  • Wear safety shoes and protective goggles at all times.
  • Do not wear loose clothes or dangling jewelry while working near machines.
  • Inspect all tools and equipment before turning them on.
  • Keep your workbench clean and dry.
  • Know the location of emergency stop buttons and fire extinguishers.
